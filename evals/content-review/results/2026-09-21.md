## Notes

- **A "clean" draft is hard to write and easy to misjudge.** The first
  replacement fixture, a write-ahead-logging topic written specifically to be
  flawless, was picked apart by a fresh reviewer on real technical grounds, and
  it had a genuine error I hadn't noticed. It was discarded. This repeats the
  2026-09-16 result, where the original control also drew real findings.
- **The control's bar was wrong, not the review.** `CR-05` demanded that a
  reviewer say "nothing worth flagging". A reviewer that never finds a single
  gap in any draft is under-scrutinizing, so that bar penalizes rigor. The
  scenario and `HOW_TO_RUN.md` now grade the control on whether the review
  reports anything **false of the text**, and treat real polish or scope
  observations as acceptable notes. This is the reframing the 2026-09-16 notes
  had recommended.
- **The draft was also tightened**, as those notes suggested: "container",
  "orchestrator" and "Kubernetes" are now defined inline and there is a worked
  numeric example. It is declared under `engineering-practices`, whose topics
  don't overlap it, though the reviewer still found the catalog duplicate, and
  observed that the subject fits that section poorly. The subject is not what the
  scenario tests, so neither observation is a failure.

  missing the closing "Where you'll meet this" section that systems topics now
  require, which a reviewer may mention in addition to the planted issue.
