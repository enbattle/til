Trigger: `docs/specs/system-design.md` — adding a new skill required a new
routing scenario (`SR-13`) per `skill-routing-eval`'s own Stage 3, plus a
regression check on the scenarios most likely to collide with its scope:

subagent with no context beyond the scenario prompt itself (never a
`fork`), instructed to state a routing decision among `/feature`,
`add-topic`, `docs-audit`, `content-audit`, `skill-routing-eval`,
`content-review-eval`, `system-design-navigation-eval`, or direct, without
implementing anything.

**5/5 PASS.** Adding `system-design-navigation-eval` did not create routing
ambiguity with any of its neighbors: it was not mistaken for `content-audit`
(a different scope, prose quality rather than whether a reader finds the right
page) or for `skill-routing-eval` (which checks which skill gets picked, not
whether readers reach the right content), and the edit to `add-topic`'s
`SKILL.md` did not change how a plain topic request routes.
